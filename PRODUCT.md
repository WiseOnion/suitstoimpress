# Suits to Impress — Product Context

## Register
brand

## Product Purpose
Demo e-commerce website for Suits to Impress, a men's formalwear store in Randolph Mall, Asheboro NC. The site is being shown to the owner (Ms. Bobbie / Dean Khiantani) as a prototype to pitch web design services. It needs to feel real, functional, and better than anything a competitor local store has — convincing enough that the owner immediately sees the value.

## Users
- Primary: Men aged 15–50 in Asheboro/Randolph County NC shopping for formal occasions
- Secondary: Parents shopping prom looks for their sons
- Demo audience: Dean Khiantani (owner), who needs to see this and say "I want this"

## Brand
- Name: Suits to Impress
- Tagline: Men's Clothing, Formalwear & Accessories
- Sub-tagline: Events · Parties · Wedding · Prom · Quinceañera
- Owner: Deepak "Dean" Khiantani, President & CEO
- Founded: 2005 (20+ years)
- BBB Rating: A+
- Social: @suitstoimpress (Instagram), @suitstoimpress2015 (Facebook)

## Visual Identity
- Colors: Black (#0a0a0a), White, Red (#cc1f1f) — ONLY these three
- Logo: "SUITS" bold + red necktie replacing the I + "TO IMPRESS" with red underline rule
- Feel: Bold, direct, confident, community-rooted. NOT luxury/editorial. NOT subtle.
- Typography: Heavy/condensed for headings (Oswald). Clean sans for body (Inter).

## Anti-references
- No gold or warm tones — wrong brand
- No delicate serif editorial style (Cormorant etc.) — too high-fashion, not them
- No SaaS aesthetic
- No gradient text
- No glassmorphism
- No identical card grids

## Occasions Served
Weddings, Prom, Homecoming, Father-Daughter Dances, Church Group Orders, Quinceañeras, Funerals, Parties, Events

## Product Categories
- Suits (including Big & Tall)
- Tuxedos
- Shoes
- Accessories (ties, belts, pocket squares, suspenders, bow ties)

## Key Differentiators
- Free in-store alterations on every purchase
- 20+ years in business
- Big & Tall sizing available
- Group orders welcome
- Walk-ins always welcome, no appointment
- Two phone numbers: (336) 629-6294 and (336) 964-1960
- Address: 1437 E Dixie Dr, Ste 435, Asheboro, NC 27203 (Randolph Mall)
- Hours: Mon-Sat 10:30AM-8:00PM, Sun 12:30PM-6:00PM

## What Should Be On The Page (Essentials)
1. Nav: Logo + links (Home, Shop, Occasions, Store) + phone CTA + bag
2. Announcement bar: free alterations callout
3. Hero: bold image + strong headline + two CTAs (Shop Now / Visit Store)
4. Shop by Occasion strip (not categories — occasions is what drives purchase intent)
5. Category grid (Suits / Tuxedos / Shoes / Accessories)
6. Featured products (4-6 items, tagged)
7. Prom/Season feature banner (timely, high-conversion)
8. Social proof strip: 4.7 stars · 45 reviews · A+ BBB · 20 years
9. Store info section: address, hours, phone, map embed placeholder
10. Footer: logo, nav links, contact info, social links, tagline

## What Should NOT Be On The Page
- Occasions 6-card grid section (too long, low conversion value for a demo)
- Shoes & Accessories as a separate section (covered in shop)
- Redundant store info (one store section is enough)
- Any text that repeats info already visible elsewhere
- Overly long hero copy
